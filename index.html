Tips for getting clean exports
  1. Ask the client for a CSV, not a PDF, from their bank covering go-live day to today. A balance column makes the check much stronger.
  2. The Bank Transactions export always asks for a date range in a pop-up and is sent to your notifications when it's ready. It ignores on-screen filters and doesn't include deleted lines.
  3. The Account Transactions export follows the on-screen filters, so clear Hide Reconciled and set the date range first.
  4. PDF statements work when they're the bank's own download, where you can select the text. Scanned or photographed statements can't be read, so ask for a CSV instead.
  5. With a PDF, a "Bank statement is complete" tick in the health checks is a good sign every line was read correctly, because it means the running balance follows on line by line.
  6. If the bank CSV has a few lines of account details above the column headings, that's fine. The tool finds the heading row.
